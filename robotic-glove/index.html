The idea in 30 seconds

Your hand already works this way

Inside your arm, muscles pull long cords called tendons, and the tendons bend your fingers. Our glove copies that.

1

The motor winds the string

Like a fishing reel. A small motor (a servo) turns a round wheel and winds up a strong fishing line.

2

The string bends the finger

The line runs along the palm side of the finger to the fingertip. Shorter string = bent finger.

3

The elastic opens it again

An elastic band on the back of the finger pulls it straight when the motor lets the string out.

A tiny computer called an Arduino tells the motor what to do: close slowly, hold, open slowly, rest, and repeat, as many times as you choose. Your laptop can start it, stop it and save the results.

Why one finger first?

A wooden finger is cheap, safe and easy to fix. If something is wrong you see it immediately. And the numbers you measure on it tell you which motor to buy for the full glove, so you don't waste money.

Part A · Build the wooden finger

Builder

  1. Cut four blocks

    From the 20 × 20 mm wooden stick, cut 80, 45, 28 and 22 mm. Sand the ends flat and round the tip of the smallest one.

    Why: these are the palm and the three bones of a finger, a little bigger than real ones.

    Watch out: cut straight. A crooked cut makes the finger bend sideways.

  2. Mark the palm side

    Draw a straight pencil line along one face of every block and write V on it. V is the palm side.

    Why: the hinges and the string go on the V side, the elastic on the other side. Marking now avoids mistakes later.

  3. Join the blocks with three small hinges

    Lay two blocks end to end, V side up, touching. Put a hinge across the joint so its round pin sits exactly over the gap. Drill small 1.5 mm holes first, then screw. Do this three times.

    Why: these are your knuckles. When the finger is straight, the blocks touch on the back, so it can't bend the wrong way, just like a real finger.

    Watch out: no pre-drilled holes = split wood. Check that each joint bends easily and doesn't wobble sideways.

  4. Screw in the little string rings

    On the V line, screw in small screw eyes: two on the base, one in the middle of each of the next two blocks, and one near the fingertip. Turn them so all the holes line up in a straight row.

    Why: the string slides through these rings. They keep it close to the finger, like the bands that hold your real tendons.

    Watch out: if the rings aren't in a straight line, the string rubs and the finger twists.

  5. Add the elastic on the back

    Put three more screw eyes on the back side and one screw on the base. Tie the elastic cord at the fingertip, run it through the eyes and wrap it twice around the screw.

    Test: bend the finger with one fingertip and let go. It should spring back straight in under a second, but still be easy to bend.

    Watch out: too tight and the motor will struggle; too loose and the finger won't open.

  6. Fix the finger to the board

    Lay the finger on its side on a wooden board and screw the base block down from below. Tape a paper protractor (angle printout) under the first joint.

    Why on its side? It bends across the board, so you can film it from above and read the angles.

  7. Make the drum (the reel)

    Glue a bottle cap (25 to 30 mm across) onto the round plastic arm that comes with the servo. Add a flat disc on each side so the string can't fall off. Drill a tiny hole near the edge to tie the string.

    Why a drum and not the arm? A round drum pulls evenly the whole way; an arm doesn't.

    Measure the cap's width and write it down. You need it later.

  8. Fix the servo to the board

    Screw the servo onto a small block of wood, 10 to 15 cm behind the finger, so the drum lines up with the string. Don't push the drum on yet; that happens in Part D.

  9. Tie the string

    Cut 60 cm of fishing line. Tie it to the fingertip ring with a strong knot plus a drop of superglue. Thread it back through every ring on the V side. Slide one bead onto the string between the two base rings; don't fix it yet.

    Pull the free end gently with your hand: the finger should curl. Let go: it should straighten. If yes, the mechanics work.

Part B · Connect the wires

Coder

Only 8 connections. Do them with everything unplugged. The yellow numbers in the picture match the list.

Never power the motor from the Arduino

The Arduino has a pin called 5V. Don't connect the servo to it. The motor's current would make the Arduino restart and could damage your laptop's USB port. The motor always gets its power from the adapter.

Before you switch on: 3 quick checks with the multimeter
  1. Beep test between + and − rails: it must not beep (no short circuit).
  2. Beep test between Arduino GND and the − rail: it must beep.
  3. Plug in the adapter only (servo unplugged), switch on: the rails should read about 5 V. Switch off: 0 V.

  5. Open and upload the test program

  6. Open the Serial Monitor

Part D · Test it together

Both

  1. Measure the pull with a luggage scale

    Motor off. Hook the luggage scale to the free end of the string. First make an ink dot on the string right where it leaves the last ring. Now pull slowly until the finger is fully bent. Read the scale (in kg) and measure how far the ink dot moved (in mm).

    Do it 3 times and use the average. Type your numbers into the motor finder just below this list.

  2. Find the two safe angles: OPEN and CLOSED

    Switch motor power on: the servo turns to 90°. Now push the drum onto the servo and tie the string to it, slightly loose. In the Serial Monitor type + or - (then Enter) to turn 2° at a time.

    OPEN = the angle where the string just becomes tight and the finger is still straight.
    CLOSED = keep going 5° at a time until the finger is bent enough, or until the motor starts to hum. Stop there.

    Write both numbers in your notebook. They are the most important numbers of the project.

  3. Fix the safety bead

    With the motor at CLOSED, slide the bead to 3 mm in front of the back ring and glue it there. Now the string can never be pulled much further than CLOSED, even if the program made a mistake.

  4. Upload the real program with your numbers

    Open robotic_glove.ino. Near the top, change these lines to your two angles, and switch off the power sensor you haven't built yet:

    const float FINGERS_OPEN_DEG   = 20;   // ← your OPEN
const float FINGERS_CLOSED_DEG = 150;  // ← your CLOSED
const bool  USE_MOTOR_SENSE  = false;  // ← false for Stage 1

    Upload it, like before.

  5. First real run

    Open the Serial Monitor. One person keeps a hand on the switch. Type each line and press Enter:

    MODE FINGERS
SPEED 20
RANGE 50
REPS 3
START

    The finger closes halfway, slowly, three times. It works? Try RANGE 100 and then SPEED 40. Type STOP anytime.

  6. The 100-times test

    Type REPS 100, SPEED 40, RANGE 100, START. It takes about 14 minutes. Every 25 times, stop and check: is the motor only warm (not hot)? Are the knots holding? Has the ink dot moved?

    If it does 100 out of 100, Stage 1 is a success.

How it works it out

Force for one finger = kg × 9.81. For four fingers it multiplies by 4, then adds 50 % for the glove fabric and friction in the tubes. The drum is sized so the motor turns 130° to pull your string distance. A motor should work at no more than 40 % of its maximum strength, so it stays cool and lasts. The finder picks the smallest motor that stays under 40 %. The thumb moves less, so a small metal-gear servo is accepted up to 60 %.

Safety

Six golden rules

Read these before you switch anything on. Say them out loud once. They're short so you remember them.

  1. Wood only, never patientsTest on the wooden finger and the wooden hand. Not on a patient, not on a friend's hand.
  2. Two people, one red switchOne person runs the test. The other keeps a hand near the switch the whole time.
  3. Motor power comes from the adapterNever from the Arduino's 5V pin, and only from a 5 V adapter.
  4. Start slow and smallAfter any change, start with SPEED 20 and RANGE 50. Go faster only when that works.
  5. The switch stops it, it doesn't open itWhen power is cut the motor freezes where it is. To free a finger, unclip or untie the string.
  6. Hot, smelly or buzzing? Switch off firstPress the switch first, think second. Then find the cause before you try again.

/*
  servo_calibration.ino  -  Stage 1 tool
  Low-Cost Robotic Assistance Glove project

  WHAT THIS IS FOR
  Moves ONE servo slowly to any angle you type in the Serial Monitor, so you
  can find the two safe limits of your finger (or glove):
     OPEN angle   = finger straight, tendon just tight (not slack, not pulling)
     CLOSED angle = the most flexion you want, before anything strains
  Write both numbers in your logbook. You will copy them into robotic_glove.ino.

  WIRING (same as the full project)
     Servo signal (orange/yellow) -> 330 ohm resistor -> D9  (D10 for the thumb)
     Servo red   (+)              -> +5 V of the EXTERNAL supply, never the Arduino 5V pin
     Servo brown (-)              -> GND of the external supply AND an Arduino GND pin

  HOW TO USE
     1. Unhook the tendon from the drum before the first upload.
     2. Upload. Open Tools > Serial Monitor, choose 115200 baud and "Newline".
     3. Type a number 0-180 and press Enter: the servo moves there slowly.
     4. Type  +  or  -  to nudge by 2 degrees.
     5. Type  s20  to set the speed to 20 degrees per second (5 to 60).
     6. Type  ?  to print the current angle.
*/

#include <Servo.h>

const uint8_t SERVO_PIN   = 9;    // change to 10 to calibrate the thumb servo
const int     START_ANGLE = 90;   // where the servo goes at power-up
const int     MIN_ANGLE   = 0;    // limits of this tool
const int     MAX_ANGLE   = 180;
const int     NUDGE_DEG   = 2;

Servo servo;
float current  = START_ANGLE;     // where the servo is now (degrees)
float target   = START_ANGLE;     // where we want it to be
float speedDps = 20.0;            // movement speed, degrees per second
unsigned long lastStepMs = 0;

char    line[16];                 // one typed command
uint8_t lineLen = 0;

void printAngle() {
  Serial.print(F("Angle now: "));
  Serial.print((int)(current + 0.5));
  Serial.print(F(" deg   (speed "));
  Serial.print((int)speedDps);
  Serial.println(F(" deg/s)"));
}

void setTarget(int deg) {
  if (deg < MIN_ANGLE) deg = MIN_ANGLE;
  if (deg > MAX_ANGLE) deg = MAX_ANGLE;
  target = deg;
  Serial.print(F("Moving to "));
  Serial.println(deg);
}

void handleLine(char *s) {
  if (s[0] == '\0') return;
  if (s[0] == '+') {
    setTarget((int)(target + 0.5) + NUDGE_DEG);
  } else if (s[0] == '-') {
    setTarget((int)(target + 0.5) - NUDGE_DEG);
  } else if (s[0] == '?') {
    printAngle();
  } else if (s[0] == 's' || s[0] == 'S') {
    float v = atof(s + 1);
    if (v >= 5 && v <= 60) {
      speedDps = v;
      printAngle();
    } else {
      Serial.println(F("Speed must be 5 to 60, e.g. s20"));
    }
  } else if (isdigit(s[0])) {
    setTarget(atoi(s));
  } else {
    Serial.println(F("Unknown. Type 0-180, +, -, s20 or ?"));
  }
}

void readSerial() {
  while (Serial.available() > 0) {
    char c = Serial.read();
    if (c == '\n' || c == '\r') {
      line[lineLen] = '\0';
      handleLine(line);
      lineLen = 0;
    } else if (lineLen < sizeof(line) - 1) {
      line[lineLen++] = c;
    }
  }
}

void setup() {
  Serial.begin(115200);
  servo.write(START_ANGLE);   // set the position BEFORE attach() so it does not jump
  servo.attach(SERVO_PIN);
  Serial.println(F("Servo calibration ready. Type 0-180, +, -, s20 or ?"));
  printAngle();
}

void loop() {
  readSerial();

  // Every 20 ms move a small step toward the target. Small steps = slow, gentle motion.
  unsigned long now = millis();
  if (now - lastStepMs >= 20) {
    float maxStep = speedDps * (now - lastStepMs) / 1000.0;
    lastStepMs = now;
    float diff = target - current;
    if (diff == 0) return;
    if (fabs(diff) <= maxStep) {
      current = target;               // arrived
      servo.write((int)(current + 0.5));
      printAngle();
    } else {
      current += (diff > 0) ? maxStep : -maxStep;
      servo.write((int)(current + 0.5));
    }
  }
}

  Low-Cost Automatic Robotic Glove for Finger Movement Assistance

  Board   : Arduino Nano (ATmega328P). Arduino IDE 2.x, built-in Servo library.
  Motors  : servo 1 pulls the four-finger tendon, servo 2 pulls the thumb tendon.

  WHAT IT DOES
   - Repeats flexion -> hold -> extension -> rest cycles automatically.
   - Speed, range of motion, repetitions, hold and rest time can be changed
     from the PC app over USB, or by editing the defaults in section 4.
   - START and STOP push buttons. Optional MODE button (D4).

// ========================= 6. MOTION =============================
int degToMicros(float deg) {
  // Servo library default: 0 deg = 544 us, 180 deg = 2400 us.
  // Microseconds give ~0.1 deg steps instead of 1 deg steps: smoother motion.
  return (int)(544.0 + deg * (2400.0 - 544.0) / 180.0 + 0.5);
}

float clampToAxis(const Axis &a, float deg) {
  float lo = (a.openDeg < a.closedDeg) ? a.openDeg : a.closedDeg;
  float hi = (a.openDeg < a.closedDeg) ? a.closedDeg : a.openDeg;
  if (deg < lo) return lo;
  if (deg > hi) return hi;
  return deg;
}

void writeAxis(Axis &a, float deg) {
  a.pos = clampToAxis(a, deg);           // THE safety clamp: every write passes here
  a.servo.writeMicroseconds(degToMicros(a.pos));
}

float flexTarget(const Axis &a) {
  return a.openDeg + (a.closedDeg - a.openDeg) * (rangePct / 100.0);
}

// Plan a smooth movement from the current position to 'target'.
void startMove(Axis &a, float target, float speed, unsigned long delayMs) {
  target    = clampToAxis(a, target);
  a.fromDeg = a.pos;
  a.toDeg   = target;
  a.durMs   = (unsigned long)(fabs(target - a.pos) / speed * 1000.0);
  if (a.durMs < 1) a.durMs = 1;
  a.startMs = millis() + delayMs;
  a.moving  = true;
}

// Called every loop: moves the servo along an S-curve (slow - faster - slow).
void updateAxis(Axis &a, unsigned long now) {
  if (!a.moving) return;
  long elapsed = (long)(now - a.startMs);
  if (elapsed < 0) return;                       // still waiting for its delay
  if ((unsigned long)elapsed >= a.durMs) {
    writeAxis(a, a.toDeg);
    a.moving = false;
    return;
  }
  float u = (float)elapsed / (float)a.durMs;      // progress 0..1
  float s = (1.0 - cos(PI * u)) / 2.0;            // smooth start and stop
  writeAxis(a, a.fromDeg + (a.toDeg - a.fromDeg) * s);
}

void runStateMachine(unsigned long now) {
  switch (state) {
    case ST_FLEX:
      if (!anyMoving()) setState(ST_HOLD);
      break;
    case ST_HOLD:
      if ((long)(now - stateStartMs) >= holdMs) beginExtend();
      break;
    case ST_EXTEND:
      if (!anyMoving()) {
        repsDone++;
        sendEvent("REP", repsDone);
        if (repsDone >= targetReps) {
          setState(ST_IDLE);
          sendEvent("DONE", repsDone);
        } else {
          setState(ST_REST);
        }
      }
      break;
    case ST_REST:
      if ((long)(now - stateStartMs) >= restMs) beginFlex();
      break;
    case ST_STOPPING:
      if (!anyMoving()) {
        setState(ST_IDLE);
        sendEvent("STOPPED", repsDone);
      }
      break;
    default:          // ST_IDLE and ST_ESTOP: nothing to do
      break;
  }
}

  }
}

void handleCommand(char *line) {
  for (char *p = line; *p; p++) *p = toupper(*p);
  char *cmd = strtok(line, " =,");
  char *arg = strtok(NULL, " =,");
  if (cmd == NULL) return;
  long val = (arg != NULL) ? atol(arg) : 0;

  if (!strcmp(cmd, "START")) {
    onStart();
  } else if (!strcmp(cmd, "STOP")) {
    requestStop();
    Serial.println(F("OK STOP"));
  } else if (!strcmp(cmd, "RESET")) {
    if (state == ST_ESTOP && motorPowerOk) rearm();
    else if (state == ST_ESTOP) Serial.println(F("ERR NO_MOTOR_POWER switch the motor supply back on first"));
    else Serial.println(F("OK RESET nothing to reset"));
  } else if (!strcmp(cmd, "STATUS")) {
    sendStatus();
  } else if (!strcmp(cmd, "PING")) {
    Serial.println(F("PONG"));
  } else if (!strcmp(cmd, "HELP")) {
    printHelp();
  } else if (strcmp(cmd, "MODE") && strcmp(cmd, "SPEED") && strcmp(cmd, "RANGE") &&
             strcmp(cmd, "REPS") && strcmp(cmd, "HOLD")  && strcmp(cmd, "REST")) {
    Serial.println(F("ERR UNKNOWN_COMMAND type HELP"));
  } else if (arg == NULL) {
    Serial.println(F("ERR MISSING_VALUE e.g. SPEED 40"));
  } else if (!strcmp(cmd, "MODE")) {
    if (!settingsAllowed()) return;
    if (!strcmp(arg, "FULL") || !strcmp(arg, "0"))         mode = MODE_FULL;
    else if (!strcmp(arg, "FINGERS") || !strcmp(arg, "1")) mode = MODE_FINGERS;
    else if (!strcmp(arg, "THUMB") || !strcmp(arg, "2"))   mode = MODE_THUMB;
    else { Serial.println(F("ERR BAD_MODE use FULL, FINGERS or THUMB")); return; }
    Serial.print(F("OK MODE "));
    Serial.println(MODE_NAMES[mode]);
  } else if (!strcmp(cmd, "SPEED")) {
    if (!settingsAllowed()) return;
    speedDps = clampLong(val, (long)SPEED_MIN_DPS, (long)SPEED_MAX_DPS);
    replyOk("SPEED", (long)speedDps);
  } else if (!strcmp(cmd, "RANGE")) {
    if (!settingsAllowed()) return;
    rangePct = clampLong(val, RANGE_MIN_PCT, RANGE_MAX_PCT);
    replyOk("RANGE", rangePct);
  } else if (!strcmp(cmd, "REPS")) {
    if (!settingsAllowed()) return;
    targetReps = clampLong(val, REPS_MIN, REPS_MAX);
    replyOk("REPS", targetReps);
  } else if (!strcmp(cmd, "HOLD")) {
    if (!settingsAllowed()) return;
    holdMs = clampLong(val, HOLD_MIN_MS, HOLD_MAX_MS);
    replyOk("HOLD", holdMs);
  } else if (!strcmp(cmd, "REST")) {
    if (!settingsAllowed()) return;
    restMs = clampLong(val, REST_MIN_MS, REST_MAX_MS);
    replyOk("REST", restMs);
  }
}

void readSerial() {
  while (Serial.available() > 0) {
    char c = Serial.read();
    if (c == '\n' || c == '\r') {
      if (rxLen > 0) {
        rx[rxLen] = '\0';
        handleCommand(rx);
        rxLen = 0;
      }
    } else if (rxLen < sizeof(rx) - 1) {
      rx[rxLen++] = c;
    }
  }
}

void loop() {
  unsigned long now = millis();

  // 1) Safety first: is the motor supply still on?
  if (now - lastSenseMs >= 5) {
    lastSenseMs = now;
    readMotorPower(now);
    if (!motorPowerOk && state != ST_ESTOP) enterEstop();
  }

  // 2) Buttons. STOP is checked before START.
  if (pressed(btnStop, now))  requestStop();
  if (pressed(btnStart, now)) onStart();
  if (pressed(btnMode, now))  nextMode();

  // 3) Commands from the PC
  readSerial();

  // 4) Move the servos and advance the exercise cycle
  if (state != ST_ESTOP) {
    updateAxis(fingers, now);
    updateAxis(thumb, now);
    runStateMachine(now);
  }

  // 5) Report to the PC and show state on the LED
  if (now - lastStatusMs >= 250) {
    lastStatusMs = now;
    sendStatus();
  }
  updateLed(now);
}

class SerialLink:
    """Opens the COM port. A background thread puts every received line in a queue."""

    def __init__(self, port, inbox):
        self.inbox = inbox
        self.ser = serial.Serial(port, BAUD, timeout=0.1)
        self.running = True
        self.thread = threading.Thread(target=self._reader, daemon=True)
        self.thread.start()

    def _reader(self):
        buffer = b""
        while self.running:
            try:
                data = self.ser.read(256)
            except (serial.SerialException, OSError) as exc:
                if self.running:
                    self.inbox.put(("LINK_LOST", str(exc)))
                return
            if not data:
                continue
            buffer += data
            while b"\n" in buffer:
                raw, buffer = buffer.split(b"\n", 1)
                text = raw.decode("ascii", errors="replace").strip()
                if text:
                    self.inbox.put(("LINE", text))

    def send(self, text):
        self.ser.write((text + "\n").encode("ascii"))

    def close(self):
        self.running = False
        self.thread.join(timeout=0.5)
        try:
            self.ser.close()
        except Exception:
            pass

#  Simulator: behaves like the Arduino firmware, for testing without hardware

class SimulatedGlove:
    OPEN = {"f": 20.0, "t": 20.0}
    CLOSED = {"f": 150.0, "t": 120.0}

    def __init__(self, inbox):
        self.inbox = inbox
        self.lock = threading.Lock()
        self.state, self.mode = "IDLE", "FULL"
        self.rep, self.reps = 0, 10
        self.speed, self.range, self.hold, self.rest = 40, 80, 1000, 1000
        self.pos = dict(self.OPEN)
        self.goal = dict(self.OPEN)
        self.move_speed = self.speed
        self.timer_end = 0.0
        self.power_on = True
        self.running = True
        self.inbox.put(("LINE", "READY ROBOTIC-GLOVE v1.0 (simulator)"))
        threading.Thread(target=self._run, daemon=True).start()

    # --- helpers -------------------------------------------------------------
    def _say(self, text):
        self.inbox.put(("LINE", text))

    def _flex_goal(self, key):
        o, c = self.OPEN[key], self.CLOSED[key]
        return o + (c - o) * self.range / 100.0

    def _set_goals(self, closing, speed):
        uses_f = self.mode != "THUMB"
        uses_t = self.mode != "FINGERS"
        self.goal["f"] = self._flex_goal("f") if (closing and uses_f) else self.OPEN["f"]
        self.goal["t"] = self._flex_goal("t") if (closing and uses_t) else self.OPEN["t"]
        self.move_speed = speed

    def _arrived(self):
        return all(abs(self.pos[k] - self.goal[k]) < 0.05 for k in self.pos)

    def _status(self):
        self._say(
            "STATUS st={} md={} rep={} reps={} f={:.1f} t={:.1f} v={} spd={} rng={} "
            "hold={} rest={}".format(
                self.state, self.mode, self.rep, self.reps, self.pos["f"], self.pos["t"],
                "5.03" if self.power_on else "0.00", self.speed, self.range,
                self.hold, self.rest))

    # --- the same state machine as the firmware ----------------------------
    def _run(self):
        last, last_status = time.time(), 0.0
        while self.running:
            time.sleep(0.02)
            now = time.time()
            dt, last = now - last, now
            with self.lock:
                if not self.power_on and self.state != "ESTOP":
                    self.state = "ESTOP"
                    self._say("EVENT ESTOP {}".format(self.rep))
                if self.state != "ESTOP":
                    for k in self.pos:
                        step = self.move_speed * dt
                        diff = self.goal[k] - self.pos[k]
                        self.pos[k] = self.goal[k] if abs(diff) <= step else self.pos[k] + step * (1 if diff > 0 else -1)
                    self._advance(now)
                if now - last_status >= 0.25:
                    last_status = now
                    self._status()

    def _advance(self, now):
        s = self.state
        if s == "FLEX" and self._arrived():
            self.state, self.timer_end = "HOLD", now + self.hold / 1000.0
        elif s == "HOLD" and now >= self.timer_end:
            self._set_goals(False, self.speed)
            self.state = "EXTEND"
        elif s == "EXTEND" and self._arrived():
            self.rep += 1
            self._say("EVENT REP {}".format(self.rep))
            if self.rep >= self.reps:
                self.state = "IDLE"
                self._say("EVENT DONE {}".format(self.rep))
            else:
                self.state, self.timer_end = "REST", now + self.rest / 1000.0
        elif s == "REST" and now >= self.timer_end:
            self._set_goals(True, self.speed)
            self.state = "FLEX"
        elif s == "STOPPING" and self._arrived():
            self.state = "IDLE"
            self._say("EVENT STOPPED {}".format(self.rep))

    # --- commands from the app ----------------------------------------------
    def send(self, text):
        words = text.strip().upper().split()
        if not words:
            return
        cmd, arg = words[0], (words[1] if len(words) > 1 else None)

                    self._set_goals(True, self.speed)

    # --- incoming messages ---------------------------------------------------------
    def _poll(self):
        try:
            for _ in range(200):
                kind, payload = self.inbox.get_nowait()
                if kind == "LINE":
                    self.handle_line(payload)
                elif kind == "LINK_LOST":
                    self.log("USB connection lost: {}".format(payload))
                    self.disconnect("cable unplugged?")
        except queue.Empty:
            pass
        if self.link:
            quiet = time.time() - self.last_line_time
            if quiet > 2.5:
                self.link_lbl.configure(text="No data from Arduino", foreground="#c0392b")
            if not self.got_ready and time.time() - self.connect_time > 5:
                self.got_ready = True      # warn only once
                self.log("No READY message yet. Is robotic_glove.ino uploaded? Baud must be 115200.")
        self.after(50, self._poll)

    def handle_line(self, line):
        self.last_line_time = time.time()
        words = line.split()
        head = words[0] if words else ""
        if head == "STATUS":
            if self.link:
                self.link_lbl.configure(text="Connected" + (" (simulator)" if self.is_sim else ""),
                                        foreground="#2e8b57")
            self.update_status(parse_fields(words[1:]))
            return
        self.log("<< " + line)
        if head == "READY":
            self.got_ready = True
            self.send("STATUS")
        elif head == "EVENT" and len(words) >= 2:
            self.handle_event(words[1], words[2:])

    def handle_event(self, name, rest):
        count = int(rest[0]) if rest and rest[0].isdigit() else 0
        if name == "START":
            self.start_session(parse_fields(rest))
        elif name == "REP" and self.session:
            self.session["reps_done"] = count
        elif name == "DONE":
            self.finish_session("COMPLETED", count)
        elif name == "STOPPED":
            self.finish_session("STOPPED", count)
        elif name == "ESTOP":
            self.finish_session("E-STOP", count)
            self.log("EMERGENCY STOP: motor power is off. Check the glove, then switch power on and press Re-arm.")
        elif name == "MODE" and rest:
            self.mode_var.set(rest[0])

    def start_session(self, cfg):
        now = datetime.now()
        self.session = {
            "test_id": self.test_id_var.get().strip() or "T?",
            "tester": self.tester_var.get().strip(),
            "notes": self.notes_var.get().strip(),
            "start": now,
            "mode": cfg.get("md", self.mode_var.get()),
            "speed": cfg.get("spd", ""), "range": cfg.get("rng", ""),
            "hold": cfg.get("hold", ""), "rest": cfg.get("rest", ""),
            "reps_target": cfg.get("reps", ""),
            "reps_done": 0,
            "min_v": float("inf"),
        }

    def finish_session(self, result, reps_done):
        if not self.session:
            return
        s, end = self.session, datetime.now()
        self.session = None
        row = {
            "test_id": s["test_id"], "tester": s["tester"],
            "date": s["start"].strftime("%Y-%m-%d"),
            "start_time": s["start"].strftime("%H:%M:%S"),
            "end_time": end.strftime("%H:%M:%S"),
            "duration_s": round((end - s["start"]).total_seconds(), 1),
            "mode": s["mode"], "speed_deg_s": s["speed"], "range_pct": s["range"],
            "hold_ms": s["hold"], "rest_ms": s["rest"],
            "reps_target": s["reps_target"], "reps_completed": max(reps_done, s["reps_done"]),
            "result": result,
            "min_motor_v": "" if s["min_v"] == float("inf") else "{:.2f}".format(s["min_v"]),
            "notes": s["notes"],
        }
        path = self.save_row(row)
        if path:
            self.log("Saved {} ({}, {} reps) to {}".format(row["test_id"], result, row["reps_completed"], path))
            self.test_id_var.set(self.next_id(row["test_id"]))

    def save_row(self, row):
        for path in (RESULTS_FILE, RESULTS_FILE.replace(".csv", datetime.now().strftime("_%H%M%S.csv"))):
            try:
                is_new = not os.path.exists(path) or os.path.getsize(path) == 0
                with open(path, "a", newline="", encoding="utf-8") as f:
                    writer = csv.DictWriter(f, fieldnames=CSV_COLUMNS)
                    if is_new:
                        writer.writeheader()
                    writer.writerow(row)
                if path != RESULTS_FILE:
                    self.log("glove_results.csv was locked (open in Excel?), saved a separate file instead.")
                return path
            except OSError as exc:
                self.log("Could not write {}: {}".format(path, exc))
        return None

    @staticmethod
    def next_id(test_id):
        """T07 -> T08, Run9 -> Run10, anything else unchanged."""
        digits = ""
        while test_id and test_id[-1].isdigit():
            digits, test_id = test_id[-1] + digits, test_id[:-1]
        if not digits:
            return test_id
        return "{}{:0{}d}".format(test_id, int(digits) + 1, len(digits))

Stuck?

Quick fixes for the usual problems

Everyone hits these the first time. Find your problem, try the fix, and change only one thing at a time.

The motor shakes or twitches

Wire 7 (Arduino GND to the − rail) is missing or loose. Check it with the beep test.

The Arduino keeps restarting

The motor is getting power from the Arduino. Move it to the adapter's rails.

Upload fails: "not in sync"

Change Tools → Processor to the other ATmega328P choice. Close the Serial Monitor. Try another cable.

No COM port appears

Install the CH340 driver. Your cable may be charge-only; try another.

The motor hums and gets hot when closed

CLOSED is too far. Make it 5 to 10° smaller. Or the elastic is too tight.

The finger doesn't open fully

The string is too tight at OPEN, or the elastic is too weak.

The motor turns but the finger hardly moves

The string is slipping on the drum or a knot is loose. Tie it through the drum hole and glue the knots.

The finger bends sideways

The string rings aren't in a straight line. Turn them back in line.

Still stuck?

Write down three things: what you expected, what actually happened, and what you already tried. Bring that to your instructor, or paste it to me. With those three, almost any problem can be solved.
